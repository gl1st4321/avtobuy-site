================================
  WELCOME TO AVTOBUY!
================================

You're a rookie car flipper. Find cars below market price, haggle, inspect,
fix them up and sell for more. The whole business lives in your phone: every
mechanic has its own app.

Below is a guide to the apps and the tricks that veterans know.


📱 APPS
-------------------------------

🛒 AvtoBuy — the car market
- A feed of listings across the whole country. Filters: price, year, city, search radius.
- Sort by "Newest first" — the juiciest deals go fast.
- Heart icon — add to favorites so you don't lose the car.
- The button in the middle — list your own car. In the listing you can say
  whether you're open to a trade.
- A listing price is at least 1,000 ₽ and no more than three times the car's
  valuation. Almost nobody will buy an overpriced car, and the game will ask
  you to confirm a price that's too low.

💬 Chats and Calls
- All haggling happens in chats. Sellers don't answer right away: some are
  online, some will reply in an hour, and some only in the morning.
- You can call to reschedule a meeting or ask to unblock a chat.

🚘 Garage
- All your cars, their parking, repairs, washing and plates.
- Parking: the yard in your home city is free (3 spots, plus one spot for each
  apartment you own), a garage and a plot by your house are free and safe,
  everything else is paid parking (money is charged every hour, even while you sleep!).
- In the yard, vandals can scratch your car. In a garage or on a plot, they can't.
- Washing: a clean car sells for slightly more for three days.
- "Backpack" — items you got from trades.

🔧 Repairs and the shop (Garage → car → "Repair")
- Car blueprint: faulty parts blink, damaged body panels are hatched.
  Tap a part and we'll show you what's broken.
- Each fault needs its own part from DetalBuy (detailing and panel beating
  don't need a part). A part only fits its own make and model.
- "At the shop" — you pay for labor at the city's hourly rate. The shop works
  from 9:00 to 20:00, and at night the car just sits. While a car is being
  repaired, you can't sell it, list it or trade it.
- "Do it yourself in the garage" — labor is free, but takes 1.6 times longer
  and only covers simple jobs (🔧 icon): battery, spark plugs, shock absorbers,
  bumper. The car must be in your garage or on your plot.
- Body: panel beating is cheaper and needs no part, but body filler remains —
  a buyer will find it with a paint gauge and pay less. Replacing the panel
  costs more, but barely hurts the price.

📦 DetalBuy — the parts store
- Pick a car and you'll see which parts it needs.
- Each part has several sellers: rating, reviews, delivery time.
  · Original — expensive and reliable.
  · Aftermarket — about half the price.
  · Used from a scrapyard — cheaper still.
  Aftermarket and used parts can break again within the first two months —
  then you repair it all over again. Flipping fast? Save money. Keeping the
  car? Buy original.
- An order takes several days to arrive (express in one day is available —
  costs more). Arrived parts wait in "Storage" until you take them to the shop.
- This is also where the flipper's tools are: a paint gauge and an OBD scanner.

📍 Transport
- A map with country borders and cities. Tap a city to see the route, price
  and time: train, plane, taxi or your own car.
- The trip is real: one hour of travel is one second on screen. You only
  arrive in the destination city when the trip ends.
- On the road you can play Drive Ace or PlameBlocks — travel time will fly
  by even faster (80 minutes per second).
- In your own car, accidents happen: breakdowns, time at the traffic police
  and sometimes a fine.

📅 Calendar, ⏰ Clock, 🌙 Sleep
- The calendar shows all your meetings. The "Set off" button opens Transport
  with the right city already selected.
- Alarms are in Clock. Sleep fast-forwards time: night sleep — until 9:00
  (available from 21:00), day sleep — once a day. Parking is charged while you sleep, too!
- In another city without your own home you'll have to sleep in a hotel.

🏦 P-Bank
- Card, balance, spending history. Your own login code.
- The paid black and gold cards give cashback on everyday spending (parking,
  travel, repairs), paid out once a week.
- Sometimes your family sends a little money "for food".

💰 PlameFinance
- Loans: the more you repay on time, the larger the amounts available to you.
  Miss a payment and you get a fine, then bailiffs take your money or your car.
- Microloans — quick, but 1% per day.
- Subsidies — a little money for watching ads.
- At a meeting you can buy a car with a car loan.

🏢 MestoBuy — real estate
- Garages, apartments and houses. Owning a home gives you spots for cars,
  free sleep and yard protection. A vacant home can be rented out.

🔢 Plates
- A nice license plate raises a car's price. The showcase refreshes every day,
  and in the "traffic police roulette" you can try your luck. You can remove,
  swap or sell your plate.

📄 Documents and contracts
- Every deal is a real contract: read it and sign on your party's
  "signature" line. Your signature is checked against your sample.
- The vehicle passport and the sales contract for your own car are on its
  card in the Garage.

🌐 Browser
- AvtoHistory — check a car by ID: liens, restrictions, tampered VIN.
- GosBuy — taxes, fines, registration and your documents (passport, license,
  vehicle passport). A purchased car must be registered within 10 days,
  or you get a fine.
- AvtoBuyCase — a casino with cases, slots and poker. Remember: the house
  always wins, and you don't.

🎮 Mini-games and side jobs
- Drive Ace — race down the highway: overtakes, near misses, coins, nitro.
  On the left are three lanes of oncoming traffic: points are tripled there,
  but cars are coming straight at you. The traffic police fine you for
  reckless driving and for crossing into oncoming lanes. You earn a little
  money per kilometer, and time passes three times faster.
- PlameBlocks — a puzzle game, time runs twice as fast, online high scores.
- Construction — a side job: 10 ₽ for every slab, up to 8 hours a day.

⚙️ Settings, 🎵 Music, 📝 Notepad, 🧮 Calculator — just like on a regular phone.
In Settings: theme, security, Dynamic Island, navigation buttons,
control sensitivity in games and progress reset.


💡 PRO SECRETS
-----------------------------------------

* REPUTATION IS EVERYTHING
You start with 5 stars. Deals raise it, missed meetings and walking out of a deal lower it.
The higher your rating, the more often buyers message you.

* HAGGLING IN CHAT
Every seller has a personality — the hint is in their first message: "price
is final", "haggling is fine", "need money urgently", "market price".
Don't open with half the price — after a couple of cheeky offers the seller
will block the chat. Trades allow up to four cars at once, but the more cars,
the less often they agree.

* MEETING AND INSPECTION
Late by more than an hour — the seller leaves. Two missed meetings in the
same chat — blocked for good.
Inspecting it yourself is free, but you'll rarely find a hidden catch. A
car picker costs 5% of the price, finds more, and measures the whole body right away.

* PAINT GAUGE
Buy one in DetalBuy — then at a meeting tap the body panels on the blueprint:
  up to 175 µm — factory paint,
  175–380 — the panel was repainted,
  above that — body filler, the car has been in a crash,
  bumpers are plastic — the gauge doesn't measure them.
The seller said "no crashes, no repaints", but the gauge shows filler? That's
the best argument for haggling. If you buy a wrecked car without checking,
buyers will find it when you sell and offer less.

* OBD SCANNER
Plug it in at a meeting — it most often catches a rolled-back odometer.

* HAGGLING AT THE HOOD
Present your arguments: faults, the catch you found, measurements, the
seller's lies, "cash right now". Ask within reason — the seller has patience:
when it runs out, the price is final. After a refusal he often offers to
split the difference — whether to accept is up to you.

* DEPOSIT
Sometimes a seller asks for a deposit — without it you can't schedule the
meeting. Sellers rated below 3.7 are often scammers: they take the money and vanish.
If you ask a buyer for a deposit yourself, schedule the meeting within three
days and don't sell the car to someone else, or the bank will block your card.

* CAN'T MAKE IT TO THE MEETING?
Send a proxy: they'll close the deal for a small fee.

* TIME KEEPS RUNNING
While the game is closed, time passes like in real life: meetings, payments
and parking won't wait. Turn on notifications and they'll remind you of important things.

Happy dealing and fat profits!
